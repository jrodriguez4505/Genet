Genet

One organism.
Many stems.

The question is not how many agents you can run. It is whether a second one is doing new work.

The idea

Most stacks treat headcount as capacity. Genet treats it as a cost. A run starts as one lead. A second worker is legal only after three gates. Quality of process over quantity of agents.

Graph

RosterLead builds the team. Only the lead changes the roster.
GoalEffect and done-state.
MemoryCues. Not a stuffed window.
ContextShared board. Isolated worker packets.
ReviewHeard and answered. Cannot take over the roster.
MethodActivate a skill first. Add a worker only after three gates.

Gates

can someone else → should we → could we. Could-we is last on purpose.
